# Moderator script: 30-minute remote session

Plan: intro 3 min, warm-up 5, tasks 15, SUS 2, debrief 5. Voice: short sentences, plain words. Never blame the person. They are the expert on their own tools.

## Before the call
- Consent answers taken (see CONSENT.md). Camera off.
- Demo account ready. Sandbox check done. Demo parcel photos ready to share as files.
- Start the recording only after the person says yes to it.

## Intro (read aloud)
"Thanks for joining. We are testing the app, not you. If something is confusing, that is the app's fault. Please say what you are thinking as you go. I may not answer right away, that is normal. You can stop or skip anything. Ready?"

## Warm-up
1. Which screen reader do you use, and on which device?
2. How do you shop online today? What works well?
3. What do you do when something arrives and you cannot tell if it is right?
4. Tell me about the last time a return or refund went wrong. What happened? What did you do?

## Tasks
Read each task as written. Do not say where to tap or what to say.

**Task 1: Buy**
"You want a navy crew-neck sweater, size M, under $40. Use the app to find one. Stop when the app reads back what it will charge. Then decide if you want to confirm."
- Done when: they reach the readback, and either confirm with the two-key step or decline on purpose.
- Note: did they hear the total, the seller and the size? Could they repeat the price?

**Task 2: Check the parcel**
"The sweater has arrived. Use the camera to check it, and tell me what the app says."
- If no props: share the demo parcel photos. Use set A (right item) and set B (wrong colour) if time allows.
- Done when: they hear the verdict and say what it means in their words.
- Note: "not sure" answers, any request to ask a helper, any point where they doubted the result.

**Task 3: Get money back**
"The parcel is wrong. You want your money back. Use the app."
- Done when: they reach a clear end state (hold voided, or case opened with the evidence pack) and say what happens next.
- Note: did they know whether money was returned, pending or requested?

## Think-aloud prompts (neutral only)
- "What are you thinking?"
- "What do you expect to happen next?"
- "What did you hear just now?"
- "Is that what you expected?"
- "Keep going, I am listening."
Do not use: "Good", "Try the button", "Did you notice...", "Why did you do that wrong".

## Assists
- Level 0: none.
- Level 1: re-read the task.
- Level 2: neutral nudge ("What options do you have here?").
- Level 3: show the step. Counts as a fail for that task.
Wait at least 20 seconds of silence before level 1, longer if the screen reader is speaking.

## What to measure
- Completion: yes / yes with assist / no, per task.
- Time: start when you finish reading the task, stop at the done condition.
- Errors: wrong action, misheard input, wrong confirmation. Note what the app said before it.
- Assists: highest level used per task.
- Quotes: exact words, with a timestamp. Mark whether permission was given.

## Debrief
1. What was the best moment? What was the worst?
2. Did you always know what you were agreeing to pay?
3. Did you trust the parcel check? When would you want a helper?
4. How did getting money back feel compared with today?
5. What would make you use this, or not?
6. Anything we did not ask that we should have?
Then thank them, explain the thank-you, and say how to reach us.
