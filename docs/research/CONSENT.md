# Consent form: Sightline remote session

You can read this form yourself or have it read aloud. You can answer each statement with "yes" or "no" out loud. You do not need to sign anything.

## What this is for
Sightline is a voice-first shopping app. It helps you buy things, check a parcel with your phone camera, and get your money back. We want to find out where it works and where it does not. We are testing the app. We are not testing you.

## What happens
- The session takes about 30 minutes, on Zoom or Meet.
- You share your screen and your screen reader audio.
- We ask a few questions about how you shop online.
- You try three short tasks: buy a sweater, check a parcel, and get money back.
- We ask for your thoughts at the end.
- You can ask the moderator for help at any time.

## Your choices
- Taking part is your choice.
- You can skip any question or task.
- You can stop at any time, for any reason. You do not have to explain.
- If you stop, nothing changes. You still get the thank-you.
- You can ask us to delete your data later. Use the contact below.

## What is recorded
- Audio of your screen reader and the app, through screen share.
- Your voice, only if you say yes below.
- No video of your face. Please keep your camera off.
- The moderator also takes written notes.

## Sandbox only
- The app uses the PayPal sandbox. It is a practice system.
- No real money moves. No card is charged.
- You use a demo account that we give you. Please do not enter any real personal details, real card numbers or real passwords.

## Quotes
- We may share short quotes in our write-up.
- We only do this with your separate permission, below.
- We use your first name or a made-up name. Your choice.

## Your data
- Notes and recordings are kept in a private folder. Only the project team can open it.
- We delete all recordings and notes with your name on them after the hackathon judging ends, on 21 December 2026.
- Results without names, such as task times and scores, may be kept in the write-up.

## Thank-you
You get a small thank-you for your time. The moderator will tell you what it is before you decide.
