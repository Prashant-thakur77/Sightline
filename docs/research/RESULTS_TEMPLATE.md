# Results template

Fill this in after the sessions. Report what happened, including what went badly.

## Rules
- State n up front, in the first line. Say how many people started and how many finished.
- Report every person and every task. No dropping sessions because they went badly. If one is excluded, say why, before you look at the result.
- Do not claim more than n supports. With small n, give counts ("4 of 6"), not percentages. No significance claims.
- Say how people were recruited and what that means for who is missing.
- Keep failures, confusing moments and "not sure" verdicts in the report.
- Every quote needs recorded permission (item 7 on the consent form). Use the name or pseudonym they chose. If permission is not on file, paraphrase and do not quote.
- Say that all sessions used the PayPal sandbox and a demo account.
- Do not describe people with pity or hero language. Report what they did and said.
